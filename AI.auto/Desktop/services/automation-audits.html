Automation Audits

A clear map of where your team's time is leaking, ranked by effort-to-savings, before you commit to a build.

Not every manual task is worth automating, and not every automation project is worth its cost. Before you spend on a build, an audit gives you an honest, ranked map of where your team's time actually goes — and which fixes pay for themselves fastest.

This isn't a sales pitch dressed up as an audit. If a task isn't worth automating yet, we'll tell you that directly.

How we deliver it

01

Interview & observe

A short call walking through your current process, plus a look at the tools involved.

02

We estimate hours spent per week on each manual task, and what automating it would realistically save.

03

Rank by ROI

Tasks are sorted by effort-to-build versus time saved, so you know exactly where to start.

04

Deliver the report

A clear, written breakdown you can act on immediately — with or without us.
